About Nonna

From Pozzuoli to your table.

Nonna Graziela grew up in Pozzuoli, on the bay just outside Naples, where putting up vegetables under olive oil is a way of life. Her pickled eggplant comes straight from that tradition: the recipe she learned at home in Southern Italy and has been making ever since.

For years it was the jar everyone reached for at holidays and Sunday dinners, and the one friends always asked to take home. Now Graziela is sharing it with everyone. Every jar is still made by hand, the same way she's always made it, with her own homemade olive oil and no shortcuts.

In Graziela's house, a jar of eggplant always has a place on the antipasto platter. Set it out next to some salami, prosciutto, sharp provolone, olives and a loaf of crusty bread, and let everyone help themselves before dinner. Don't forget to drizzle a little of the olive oil from the jar over the top.

Con amore, Nonna Graziela
